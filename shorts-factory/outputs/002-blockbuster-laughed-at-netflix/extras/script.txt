#002 — Blockbuster Could Have Bought Netflix for $50M

NARRATION
In the year 2000, Netflix was losing money, and its founders flew to Dallas with an offer.
They offered Netflix to Blockbuster, the king of video rentals, for fifty million dollars.
According to co-founder Marc Randolph, Blockbuster's executives could barely keep a straight face.
They said no.
Ten years later, Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy.
Netflix is now worth hundreds of billions. And only one Blockbuster store is left, in Oregon.
