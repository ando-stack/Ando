#011 — Samsung Started Out Selling Dried Fish 🐟

NARRATION
Your Samsung phone comes from a shop that sold dried fish.
In 1938, Lee Byung-chul founded Samsung in Daegu, Korea.
It was a tiny trading company selling dried fish, vegetables and noodles.
Then it moved into sugar, textiles, insurance…
And at the end of the sixties, it got into electronics.
Today, Samsung is one of the biggest makers of phones and chips on Earth. Is there a Samsung near you right now?
