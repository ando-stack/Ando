#042 — Toyota Started by Making Weaving Looms

NARRATION
Toyota didn't start with cars. It started with weaving machines.
Sakichi Toyoda was an inventor who built automatic looms in Japan.
His best loom stopped by itself when a thread broke. It was a huge deal.
In 1929, the rights to that loom were sold to a British company.
His son Kiichiro used that money to help start building cars.
Today, Toyota is one of the biggest car makers in the world. All thanks to a loom.
