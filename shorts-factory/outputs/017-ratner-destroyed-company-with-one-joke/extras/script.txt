#017 — He Lost £500 Million With One Joke

NARRATION
This man destroyed his own company with one joke.
In 1991, Gerald Ratner ran the biggest jewelry chain in Britain.
In a speech, he joked that some of his products were total crap.
His customers heard it. And they stopped buying.
About five hundred million pounds was wiped off the company's value, and Ratner was soon out.
In Britain, it's still called doing a Ratner. Careful what you say.
