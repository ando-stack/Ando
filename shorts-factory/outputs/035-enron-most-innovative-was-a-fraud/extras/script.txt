#035 — America's 'Most Innovative Company' Was a Fraud

NARRATION
For six years in a row, Fortune called Enron America's most innovative company.
It was one of the biggest energy companies in the United States.
But a lot of its profits weren't real. Debts were hidden in side companies with clever accounting.
In 2001, the truth came out. The stock went from around ninety dollars to under one dollar.
Enron went bankrupt. Thousands lost their jobs and retirement savings.
The most innovative thing about it was the accounting.
