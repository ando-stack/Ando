#046 — Walt Disney Went Bankrupt Before Mickey Mouse

NARRATION
Before Mickey Mouse, Walt Disney went bankrupt.
As the story goes, an early boss told him he lacked imagination.
In Kansas City, he started his first animation studio, Laugh-O-Gram. In 1923, it went bankrupt.
He moved to Hollywood with very little money and a suitcase.
In 1928, he released a cartoon with a new character: Mickey Mouse.
Today, Disney is one of the biggest entertainment companies on Earth. Failure isn't the end.
