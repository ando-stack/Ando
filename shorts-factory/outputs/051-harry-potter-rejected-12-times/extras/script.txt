#051 — Harry Potter Was Rejected 12 Times 📚

NARRATION
Harry Potter was rejected by twelve publishers.
J. K. Rowling was a single mother with very little money when she finished the first book.
Publisher after publisher said no.
Then a small publisher, Bloomsbury, gave the first chapter to the boss's eight year old daughter. She wanted more.
They published it in 1997, with a small advance.
The series has sold over five hundred million copies. Twelve people said no. One kid said yes.
