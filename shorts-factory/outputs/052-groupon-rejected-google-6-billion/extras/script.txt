#052 — Groupon Said No to $6 Billion From Google

NARRATION
In 2010, Google offered about six billion dollars for a coupon website.
It was Groupon, one of the fastest growing companies in history.
Groupon said no. They thought they'd be worth even more alone.
At first, it looked smart. Its stock market debut in 2011 valued it at over ten billion dollars.
Then growth stalled, copycats appeared, and the stock crashed.
Today Groupon is worth a small fraction of that offer. Would you have sold?
