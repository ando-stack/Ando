#053 — Snapchat Turned Down $3 Billion in Cash

NARRATION
At twenty three, he turned down three billion dollars in cash.
In 2013, Snapchat had no revenue, but millions of teens were using it.
Facebook offered to buy it for three billion dollars.
CEO Evan Spiegel said no. People called him crazy.
In 2017, Snap went public, valued at over twenty billion dollars.
Sometimes saying no is the best deal. Would you have taken the money?
