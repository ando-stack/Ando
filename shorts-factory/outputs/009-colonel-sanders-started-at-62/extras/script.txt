#009 — He Started KFC at 62 🍗

NARRATION
If you think it's too late to start, listen to this.
Harland Sanders spent his life switching jobs. Railroad worker, salesman, gas station operator.
In 1952, at sixty two, he started selling his fried chicken recipe to other restaurants.
A few years later, a new highway took the traffic away from his own restaurant.
So he went all in, driving from restaurant to restaurant to sell his recipe. He heard no, again and again.
Today, KFC is in more than one hundred forty countries.
It's never too late.
