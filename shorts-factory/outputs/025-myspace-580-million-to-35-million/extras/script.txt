#025 — MySpace Went From $580M to $35M

NARRATION
In 2005, MySpace was the biggest social network in the world.
Rupert Murdoch's News Corp bought it for five hundred and eighty million dollars.
Then Facebook showed up. Cleaner, faster, simpler.
Users left by the millions.
In 2011, MySpace sold for about thirty five million.
It lost over ninety percent of its value in six years. On the internet, nobody is untouchable.
