#048 — A Milkshake Machine Salesman Took Over McDonald's

NARRATION
McDonald's wasn't founded by the man who made it famous.
In 1954, Ray Kroc was fifty two and selling milkshake machines.
One restaurant in California ordered eight of them. He had to see why.
It was run by the McDonald brothers, with a super fast kitchen system.
Kroc started franchising it. In 1961, he bought the brothers out for two point seven million dollars.
Today McDonald's is one of the biggest restaurant chains on the planet. The brothers' name stayed. They didn't.
