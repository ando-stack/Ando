#006 — Nokia Had It All… and Lost It in 6 Years

NARRATION
In 2007, almost half of all phones sold in the world were Nokias.
They were indestructible. Everyone had one.
That same year, the iPhone came out. A touchscreen with no keys? Many thought it was a fad.
They were wrong.
In 2013, Nokia sold its phone business to Microsoft for about five point four billion euros, a fraction of its old value.
Did you have a Nokia?
