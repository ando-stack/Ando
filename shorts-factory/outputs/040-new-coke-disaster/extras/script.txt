#040 — Coca-Cola Changed Its Recipe… and People Revolted

NARRATION
In 1985, Coca-Cola changed its secret recipe.
Pepsi was winning taste tests, so Coke made a sweeter formula. In blind tests, people liked it.
But when it hit stores, people were furious. They wanted the original back.
The company got a flood of angry calls and letters. Some fans stockpiled the old Coke.
After just seventy nine days, the original returned as Coca-Cola Classic.
Sales went up. Some still wonder if it was all a genius plan. What do you think?
