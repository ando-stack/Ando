#001 — He Could Have Bought Google for $750K 😳

NARRATION
This man could have bought Google… and he said no.
In 1999, Larry Page and Sergey Brin wanted to sell Google and go back to Stanford.
They offered it to George Bell, the CEO of Excite, one of the biggest search engines back then.
The price? Seven hundred and fifty thousand dollars.
Bell thought it was too expensive. He turned it down.
Today, Google is worth over two trillion dollars.
And Excite? Almost nobody remembers it. Would you have bought it?
