#004 — He Paid 10,000 Bitcoin for 2 Pizzas 🍕

NARRATION
These are the most expensive pizzas in history.
On May twenty second, 2010, a programmer named Laszlo Hanyecz wanted to see if bitcoin could actually buy something.
He offered ten thousand bitcoin to anyone who would bring him two pizzas. Someone said yes.
Back then, that was about forty dollars.
At bitcoin's peak, those coins were worth more than one billion dollars.
Today, May twenty second is Bitcoin Pizza Day. Would you have eaten them?
