#058 — Ben & Jerry's Started With a $5 Course 🍦

NARRATION
Ben and Jerry's started with a five dollar course.
Ben Cohen and Jerry Greenfield were childhood friends who wanted to start a food business.
They split the cost of a five dollar mail order course on making ice cream.
In 1978, with about twelve thousand dollars, they opened a shop in an old gas station in Vermont.
Big chunks, funny names, and free ice cream days made people love them.
In 2000, Unilever bought the company for three hundred and twenty six million dollars.
