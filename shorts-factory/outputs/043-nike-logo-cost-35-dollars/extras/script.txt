#043 — The Nike Logo Cost $35 ✔️

NARRATION
One of the most famous logos on Earth cost thirty five dollars.
In 1971, Phil Knight needed a logo for his new shoe brand.
He asked Carolyn Davidson, a design student, to make one.
She drew the swoosh. Knight said: I don't love it, but it will grow on me.
Years later, Nike gave her a gold ring with the swoosh and shares in the company.
Today that swoosh is worth billions. Would you take thirty five dollars for it?
