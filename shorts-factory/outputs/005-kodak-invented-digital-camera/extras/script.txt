#005 — Kodak Invented the Digital Camera… and It Killed Them

NARRATION
Kodak invented the digital camera. And that's what destroyed it.
In 1975, a Kodak engineer named Steve Sasson built the very first digital camera.
But Kodak was making a fortune selling film.
Digital cameras threatened that business, so Kodak never fully bet on them.
Other companies did.
In 2012, Kodak filed for bankruptcy.
Sometimes a company's biggest enemy is its own success.
