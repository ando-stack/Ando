#038 — Slack Came From a Failed Video Game

NARRATION
Slack exists because a video game failed.
Stewart Butterfield's company was building an online game called Glitch.
Not enough people played it. In 2012, they shut it down.
But while making it, the team had built their own internal chat tool.
They turned that tool into a product and called it Slack.
In 2021, Salesforce bought it for twenty seven point seven billion dollars. Look at what you build along the way.
