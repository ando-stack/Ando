#047 — He Bought Starbucks for $3.8 Million

NARRATION
Starbucks used to sell coffee beans, not coffee drinks.
In 1983, an employee named Howard Schultz visited Italy and fell in love with espresso bars.
He wanted Starbucks to sell espresso drinks. The owners weren't interested.
So he left and opened his own coffee shops.
In 1987, he bought Starbucks itself for about three point eight million dollars.
Today there are tens of thousands of Starbucks around the world.
