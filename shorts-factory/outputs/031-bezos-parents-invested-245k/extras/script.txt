#031 — His Parents Bet $245,000 on Amazon

NARRATION
In 1995, Jeff Bezos asked his parents for money for a tiny online bookstore.
He warned them: there's about a seventy percent chance you lose all of it.
They invested anyway. Around two hundred and forty five thousand dollars.
That bookstore was Amazon.
That bet turned into billions of dollars for the Bezos family.
Would you have bet on your kid?
