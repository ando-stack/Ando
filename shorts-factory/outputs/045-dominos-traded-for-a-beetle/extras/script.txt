#045 — He Traded Half of Domino's for a Used Car 🚗

NARRATION
This man traded half of Domino's for a used car.
In 1960, brothers Tom and James Monaghan bought a small pizzeria in Michigan.
Pretty soon, James wanted out.
So he gave Tom his half of the business… in exchange for a Volkswagen Beetle.
Tom kept going, renamed it Domino's, and built it into a giant.
In 1998, he sold most of it for about one billion dollars. Would you have kept the car?
