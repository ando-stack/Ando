#026 — $1.75 Billion App Dead in 6 Months

NARRATION
This company raised one point seven five billion dollars and died in about six months.
It was called Quibi. Hollywood-quality shows in ten minute episodes, made only for your phone.
It had stars, famous directors, and a huge budget.
It launched in April 2020, right when everyone was stuck at home, with no commute to fill.
And you couldn't even share clips on social media.
In October, it announced it was shutting down. Money can't buy success.
