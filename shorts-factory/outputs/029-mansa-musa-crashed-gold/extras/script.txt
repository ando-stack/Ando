#029 — He Was So Rich He Crashed the Price of Gold 👑

NARRATION
The richest person in history wasn't Elon Musk.
Many historians say it was Mansa Musa, king of the Mali Empire, in the fourteenth century.
In 1324, he traveled to Mecca with a huge caravan carrying gold.
When he passed through Cairo, he spent and gave away so much gold…
that gold lost value in the city for years.
He was so rich, nobody can really calculate his fortune.
