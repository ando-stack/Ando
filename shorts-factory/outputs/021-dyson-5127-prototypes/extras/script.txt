#021 — He Failed 5,126 Times Before Getting Rich

NARRATION
This man failed five thousand one hundred and twenty six times.
James Dyson was annoyed that his vacuum lost power as the bag filled up.
So he tried to build a vacuum with no bag at all.
For about five years, he built prototype after prototype. He went deep into debt.
Prototype number five thousand one hundred and twenty seven worked.
Today Dyson is a global brand. How many times would you have tried?
