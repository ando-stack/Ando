#060 — One Line of Text Made Hotmail Worth $400 Million

NARRATION
One line of text made this startup worth four hundred million dollars.
In 1996, Hotmail launched one of the first free email services you could use from any browser.
At the bottom of every email, they added a line: get your free email at Hotmail.
So every user became an ad. Friends signed up, then their friends.
It grew to millions of users incredibly fast.
In late 1997, Microsoft bought Hotmail for about four hundred million dollars. The best marketing can be free.
