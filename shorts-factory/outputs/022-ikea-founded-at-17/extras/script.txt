#022 — IKEA Was Started by a 17-Year-Old

NARRATION
IKEA was started by a seventeen year old.
As a kid in Sweden, Ingvar Kamprad sold matches to his neighbors from his bike.
In 1943, he started his own company. The name is made of his initials, his family farm, and his village.
Ingvar Kamprad, Elmtaryd, Agunnaryd.
First he sold pens and wallets. Then furniture.
And the big idea came when an employee took the legs off a table to fit it in a car. Flat-pack furniture was born.
