#019 — Michael Jordan Almost Signed With Adidas 👟

NARRATION
Michael Jordan didn't want to sign with Nike. He wanted Adidas.
In 1984, Nike was mostly a running brand, not a basketball one.
Jordan didn't even want to go to the meeting. His mother convinced him.
Nike offered something almost no one got: his own shoe, and a cut of every pair sold.
That shoe was the Air Jordan. Today the Jordan brand brings in billions of dollars a year.
And Jordan still gets paid on every pair. Would you have taken the meeting?
