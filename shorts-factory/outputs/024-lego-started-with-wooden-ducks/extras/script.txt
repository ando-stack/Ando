#024 — LEGO Started With Wooden Ducks 🦆

NARRATION
LEGO started with wooden ducks.
In 1932, a Danish carpenter named Ole Kirk Christiansen was struggling to find work.
So he made wooden toys. The name comes from the Danish leg godt, which means play well.
In 1942, a fire burned his factory down. He rebuilt it.
Then he bet on plastic bricks, when almost nobody believed in plastic toys.
Today, LEGO is one of the biggest toy companies on Earth.
