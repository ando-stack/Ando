#041 — Wrigley's Gum Started as a Free Gift 🍬

NARRATION
Wrigley's gum started as a free gift.
In 1891, William Wrigley Junior moved to Chicago to sell soap.
To sell more, he gave away free baking powder with each order.
The baking powder was more popular than the soap. So he sold that, and gave away free chewing gum.
Then people wanted the gum more than the baking powder.
So he sold gum, and Wrigley became one of the biggest gum companies in the world. Watch what customers actually want.
