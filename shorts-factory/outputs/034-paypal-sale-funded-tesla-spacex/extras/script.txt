#034 — The Sale That Paid for Tesla and SpaceX

NARRATION
In 2002, eBay bought PayPal for one and a half billion dollars.
One of its biggest shareholders, Elon Musk, got around one hundred and eighty million.
Most people would retire. He didn't.
He put almost all of it into two companies everyone thought would fail: a rocket company and an electric car company.
In 2008, both were close to running out of money.
Both survived. SpaceX and Tesla made him one of the richest people on Earth. Would you have risked it all?
