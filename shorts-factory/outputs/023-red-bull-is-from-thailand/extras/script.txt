#023 — Red Bull Is Actually From Thailand 🐂

NARRATION
Red Bull wasn't invented in Austria.
Dietrich Mateschitz was selling toothpaste. On a trip to Thailand, he tried a drink truck drivers used to stay awake.
It was called Krating Daeng. In Thai, that means red bull.
He partnered with its creator, Chaleo Yoovidhya, and changed the recipe for western tastes.
In 1987, Red Bull launched in Austria, with almost no normal ads. Just extreme sports and wild events.
Today it sells billions of cans every year.
