#055 — Segway Was Supposed to Change the World

NARRATION
This invention was supposed to change how cities work.
In 2001, before anyone had even seen it, it was one of the most hyped products in tech. Its secret code name was Ginger.
Some famous tech leaders reportedly said it could be as big as the personal computer.
Then it was revealed: the Segway, a self-balancing scooter.
It cost thousands of dollars, and many cities didn't know where to allow it. Sales were far below the hype.
In 2020, the original Segway stopped being made. Hype is not a business plan.
