#037 — Twitter Was a Side Project of a Dying Startup

NARRATION
Twitter was born because Apple killed a startup.
In 2005, a company called Odeo was building a podcast platform.
Then Apple put podcasts right inside iTunes. Odeo was finished.
So the team looked for a new idea. One of them proposed short status updates by text message.
In 2006, it became Twitter.
In 2022, Elon Musk bought it for forty four billion dollars. One failure can start something huge.
