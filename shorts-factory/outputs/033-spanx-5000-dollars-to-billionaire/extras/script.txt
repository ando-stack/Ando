#033 — From $5,000 to Billionaire: Spanx

NARRATION
She cut the feet off her pantyhose, and became a billionaire.
Sara Blakely was selling fax machines door to door.
She wanted something smooth to wear under white pants, so she grabbed scissors and made it herself.
With five thousand dollars of savings, she started Spanx in 2000.
Factories laughed at her idea. One finally said yes.
In 2012, Forbes called her the youngest self-made female billionaire in the world.
