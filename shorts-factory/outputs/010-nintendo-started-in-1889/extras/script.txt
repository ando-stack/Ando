#010 — Nintendo Started in 1889 Selling Playing Cards 🃏

NARRATION
Nintendo is older than you think. Much older.
It was founded in 1889, in Kyoto, Japan.
But it didn't make video games. It made playing cards called hanafuda.
In the sixties, it tried anything to survive. A taxi company. Even instant rice.
Almost all of it failed.
Then it bet on toys, and later on video games. Today it's one of the biggest names in entertainment.
