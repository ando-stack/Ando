#030 — Apple Fired Steve Jobs… Then Paid $429M to Get Him Back

NARRATION
Steve Jobs was pushed out of the company he started.
In 1985, after a power struggle with Apple's CEO, the board sided against him. He left.
He started a new computer company called NeXT, and bought a small animation studio called Pixar.
Meanwhile, Apple was in serious trouble.
So Apple bought NeXT for four hundred and twenty nine million dollars, and Jobs came back.
Then came the iPod, the iPhone, and one of the most valuable companies on Earth. Getting fired can be the start.
