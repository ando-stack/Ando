#018 — The Record Label That Rejected The Beatles 🎸

NARRATION
On New Year's Day, 1962, an unknown band auditioned for Decca Records.
They recorded fifteen songs in about an hour.
Decca said no. As the story goes, they were told guitar groups were on their way out.
That band was The Beatles.
Months later, they signed with another label and became the best-selling band in history.
How many opportunities do we turn down without knowing it?
