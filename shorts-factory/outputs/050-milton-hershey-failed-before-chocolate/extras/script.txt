#050 — Hershey Failed Again and Again Before Chocolate 🍫

NARRATION
The man behind Hershey's chocolate failed again and again.
Milton Hershey started a candy business in Philadelphia. It failed.
He tried again in New York. It failed too.
Back home in Pennsylvania, he started making caramels. This time, it worked.
In 1900, he sold the caramel company for one million dollars and went all in on chocolate.
He built a whole town around his chocolate factory. Hershey, Pennsylvania.
