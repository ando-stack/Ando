#057 — Atari Buried Its Failed Game in the Desert

NARRATION
Atari buried its failed games in the desert.
In 1982, Atari paid a fortune for the rights to make a video game of E.T.
The game was made in about five weeks to hit Christmas.
Players hated it. Millions of cartridges didn't sell.
In 1983, trucks dumped unsold games in a landfill in New Mexico. For years, people called it a legend.
In 2014, they dug it up. The games were really there. Rushing can cost you everything.
