#056 — The $75,000 Deal That Made Bill Gates Rich

NARRATION
The deal that made Bill Gates rich used software he didn't write.
In 1980, IBM needed an operating system for its first personal computer.
Microsoft didn't have one. So it bought one from a small Seattle company for about seventy five thousand dollars.
They adapted it and licensed it to IBM. But here's the genius part: Microsoft kept the rights.
When other companies copied the IBM PC, they all needed that operating system. And Microsoft sold it to all of them.
That's how Microsoft took over the PC world. Read the contract.
