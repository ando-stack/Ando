#059 — Canva Was Rejected by Over 100 Investors

NARRATION
Investors said no to her more than one hundred times.
Melanie Perkins was a university student in Australia teaching people to use design software.
It was too hard to learn. So she imagined a simple design tool anyone could use online.
She pitched investors again and again. Most rejected her.
She kept going. In 2013, Canva launched.
Today Canva has millions of users and is worth tens of billions of dollars. One yes is enough.
