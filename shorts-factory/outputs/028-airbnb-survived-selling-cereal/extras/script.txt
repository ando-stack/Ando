#028 — Airbnb Survived by Selling Cereal 🥣

NARRATION
Airbnb was saved by boxes of cereal.
In 2008, its founders were in debt, and almost nobody was using their website.
It was election year in America. So they designed cereal boxes for the candidates: Obama O's and Cap'n McCain's.
They sold them as collector's items.
They made about thirty thousand dollars. Just enough to keep going.
Today Airbnb is worth tens of billions. Sometimes, to survive, you have to get creative.
