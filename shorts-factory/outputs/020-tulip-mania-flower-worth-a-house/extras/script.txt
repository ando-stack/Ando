#020 — When One Flower Cost as Much as a House 🌷

NARRATION
In the sixteen hundreds, in Holland, a single tulip bulb could cost as much as a house.
Tulips were the ultimate luxury. Everyone wanted them.
People started buying bulbs just to sell them for more a few days later.
According to accounts from the time, the rarest bulbs reached thousands of guilders.
Then, in February 1637, buyers simply stopped showing up. Prices collapsed.
It's often called the first bubble in history. Sound familiar?
