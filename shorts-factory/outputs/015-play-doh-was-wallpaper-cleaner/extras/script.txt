#015 — Play-Doh Was Originally a Wallpaper Cleaner 🤯

NARRATION
The modeling clay you played with as a kid was a cleaning product.
In the 1930s, a company called Kutol sold a putty to clean soot off wallpaper.
But home heating changed, people stopped buying it, and the company was about to go under.
Then a teacher noticed kids could use it to make shapes.
They removed the detergent, added colors, and Play-Doh was born.
Billions of cans have been sold since.
