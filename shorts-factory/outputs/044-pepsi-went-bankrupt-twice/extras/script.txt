#044 — Pepsi Went Bankrupt Twice

NARRATION
Pepsi went bankrupt. Twice.
The first time was in 1923, after sugar prices went crazy.
It was sold, and in 1931 it went bankrupt again.
Then Charles Guth, the boss of a candy store chain, bought it. As the story goes, Pepsi was even offered to Coca-Cola. Coke said no.
Pepsi survived by selling bigger bottles for the same price: twice as much for a nickel.
Today, it's one of the biggest food and drink companies in the world.
