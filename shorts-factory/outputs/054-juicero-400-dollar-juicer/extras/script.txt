#054 — The $400 Juicer You Could Squeeze by Hand

NARRATION
This startup raised around one hundred and twenty million dollars for a juicer.
It was called Juicero. A Wi-Fi juice press that squeezed special packs of chopped fruit.
It launched at almost seven hundred dollars, then dropped to four hundred.
Then reporters showed you could squeeze the packs with your bare hands, just as fast.
The internet laughed. A few months later, in 2017, Juicero shut down.
Lesson: make sure your product does something your hands can't.
