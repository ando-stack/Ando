#032 — He Sold an Internet Radio Site for $5.7 Billion

NARRATION
Mark Cuban sold a website for five point seven billion dollars. At the perfect moment.
His company, Broadcast.com, streamed radio and sports over the internet. In the nineties, that was magic.
In 1999, at the peak of the dot com craze, Yahoo bought it, paying in stock.
Cuban protected his shares so a crash couldn't wipe him out.
Months later, the bubble burst, and Yahoo eventually shut Broadcast.com down.
Cuban walked away a billionaire. Timing is everything.
