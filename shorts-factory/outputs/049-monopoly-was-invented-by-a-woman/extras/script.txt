#049 — Monopoly's Real Inventor Got $500

NARRATION
The real inventor of Monopoly got just five hundred dollars.
In 1904, Lizzie Magie patented a board game called The Landlord's Game.
Its goal was to show how monopolies make landlords rich and everyone else poor.
Decades later, a man named Charles Darrow sold a version of it to Parker Brothers as Monopoly.
Darrow became a millionaire. Parker Brothers bought Magie's patent for five hundred dollars.
A game about unfair wealth… with an unfair ending.
