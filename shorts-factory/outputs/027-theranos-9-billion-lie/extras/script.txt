#027 — She Fooled Everyone With One Drop of Blood 🩸

NARRATION
She convinced the world she had a revolutionary invention. It didn't work.
Elizabeth Holmes dropped out of Stanford and started Theranos at nineteen.
She promised hundreds of medical tests from a single drop of blood.
Famous investors poured in money. At its peak, the company was valued at nine billion dollars.
But the machines didn't do what she claimed.
In 2022, she was convicted of fraud and sentenced to more than eleven years in prison.
