#008 — The Founder of Zara Started as a Delivery Boy

NARRATION
One of the richest men in the world started out delivering shirts.
Amancio Ortega left school at fourteen and went to work at a shirt shop in Spain.
Later, with his family, he started making bathrobes.
In 1975, he opened a small store in A Coruña. He called it Zara.
His idea was simple: copy trends fast and have new clothes in stores every few weeks.
Today his company, Inditex, is one of the biggest fashion empires on the planet.
