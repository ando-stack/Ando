#007 — Lamborghini Exists Because Ferrari Insulted Him 🐂

NARRATION
Lamborghini exists because Enzo Ferrari insulted a tractor maker.
Ferruccio Lamborghini got rich building tractors, and he owned several Ferraris.
As the story goes, he complained to Enzo Ferrari about a clutch problem.
And Ferrari basically told him: stick to your tractors.
Lamborghini took it personally. In 1963, he started his own supercar company to beat Ferrari.
Never underestimate an angry man with money.
