#012 — Facebook Rejected Him… Then Paid $19 Billion for His App

NARRATION
In 2009, Facebook turned this man down for a job.
His name was Brian Acton. He tweeted that it was a great opportunity, and he was moving on.
That same year, he joined his friend Jan Koum on a small messaging app called WhatsApp.
Five years later, Facebook bought it for nineteen billion dollars.
The most expensive rejection in history.
