#014 — 13 Employees. $1 Billion. Instagram

NARRATION
In 2012, a company with just thirteen employees sold for one billion dollars.
It was Instagram. It was less than two years old and had barely made any money.
Mark Zuckerberg bought it anyway.
People called it insane.
Today Instagram has over two billion users and makes billions every year.
It wasn't insane. It was one of the best deals ever made.
