#036 — Google Used to Be Called BackRub

NARRATION
Google's first name was… BackRub.
In 1996, Larry Page and Sergey Brin built a search engine at Stanford that ranked pages by their backlinks.
Back links. BackRub. That was the name.
Then they wanted something bigger. They chose googol, the number one followed by one hundred zeros.
As the story goes, it was misspelled, and Google stuck.
Would you search on BackRub?
