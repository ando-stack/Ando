#016 — Warren Buffett's $200 Billion Revenge Mistake

NARRATION
The greatest investor in the world made a two hundred billion dollar mistake… out of pride.
In the early sixties, Warren Buffett was buying shares of a textile company called Berkshire Hathaway.
The boss promised to buy his shares back at one price, then offered a little less.
Buffett was so angry, he bought control of the whole company and fired him.
But it was a dying textile business. Buffett later said that revenge cost him around two hundred billion dollars.
Lesson: never invest angry.
