#003 — He Sold 10% of Apple for $800

NARRATION
Apple wasn't founded by two people. It was founded by three.
In 1976, Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne started Apple. Wayne got ten percent.
But he was scared of the company's debts.
So just twelve days later, he sold his share for eight hundred dollars.
That ten percent would be worth billions today.
Wayne says he has no regrets. Do you believe him?
