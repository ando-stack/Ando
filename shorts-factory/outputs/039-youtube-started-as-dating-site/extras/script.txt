#039 — YouTube Started as a Dating Site 😳

NARRATION
The site you're watching right now started as a dating website.
In early 2005, YouTube's founders had an idea: people upload videos about themselves to find a date.
Nobody uploaded dating videos.
So they opened it up to any video at all.
Around a year and a half later, Google bought YouTube for one point six five billion dollars.
Today, billions of people use it. Sometimes the first idea is just the door.
